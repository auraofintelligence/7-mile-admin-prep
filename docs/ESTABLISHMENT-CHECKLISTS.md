# Establishment checklists

All statuses are to do. Completion requires evidence.

## Founding decisions

Set the people, purposes, control and land evidence before adopting documents.

- [ ] T01 Confirm purposes and two-entity working structure
- [ ] T02 Agree cultural decision and permission process
- [ ] T03 Record exact lot/plan, tenure and access evidence
- [ ] T04 Choose not-for-profit membership and director rules
- [ ] T08 Choose share ownership, votes and durable control

## Not-for-profit registration

The ORIC working option is reviewed and adopted by the founding members. Charity eligibility is assessed separately.

- [ ] T05 Prepare ORIC not-for-profit rule book
- [ ] T06 Collect private membership consents
- [ ] T07 Prepare private director consents and eligibility records
- [ ] T11 Assemble review pack and ask about QYAC-assisted review
- [ ] T12 Resolve rule book and company document comments
- [ ] T13 Hold founding ORIC meeting or circulating consent process
- [ ] T14 Lodge ORIC application and record determination
- [ ] T17 Assess charity eligibility and DGR separately
- [ ] T18 Apply for ABNs, TFNs and relevant tax registrations

## Commercial registration

Share ownership, votes and durable Indigenous control are documented together.

- [ ] T08 Choose share ownership, votes and durable control
- [ ] T09 Draft commercial constitution
- [ ] T10 Draft shareholders agreement
- [ ] T12 Resolve rule book and company document comments
- [ ] T15 Complete registered office, shares and ASIC registration inputs
- [ ] T16 Register commercial company and issue initial shares
- [ ] T18 Apply for ABNs, TFNs and relevant tax registrations

## Relationships and financial administration

Separate accounts, competent approvals and an explicit service agreement support the two entities.

- [ ] T19 Draft inter-entity responsibilities and community benefit terms
- [ ] T20 Confirm Ready S.E.T. Co-op legal identity and service scope
- [ ] T21 Draft administration, employment and training service agreement
- [ ] T22 Adopt conflict, related-party and delegation policy
- [ ] T23 Open separate bank accounts and appoint signatories
- [ ] T24 Set up chart of accounts, payroll and monthly close in-house
- [ ] T25 Build regulator and operating compliance calendar

## Land and development pathway

The exact parcel and actual uses determine the approval pathway. Temporary does not establish exemption.

- [ ] T26 Check current zoning, overlays and existing approvals
- [ ] T27 Describe day activities, residency and temporary stays separately
- [ ] T28 Request Council pre-lodgement guidance against the parcel
- [ ] T29 Screen SARA state referral triggers
- [ ] T30 Confirm cultural heritage process for site activities
- [ ] T31 Screen national environmental matters and referral need
- [ ] T32 Check survey, access, fire, water and wastewater capacity
- [ ] T33 Define staged infrastructure and development application scope
- [ ] T34 Prepare and lodge required development/works applications

## Cultural activity pilot

Begin with culturally approved offerings and evidenced demand and operating requirements.

- [ ] T35 Select culturally approved pilot experiences
- [ ] T36 Collect evidence of cultural activity demand and pricing
- [ ] T37 Draft cultural activities business plan
- [ ] T42 Prepare visitor terms and operating handbook
- [ ] T43 Prepare emergency plan, risk register and insurance scope
- [ ] T44 Prepare paid roles, volunteering boundaries and training plan
- [ ] T45 Agree media, ICIP, privacy and AI permissions

## Accommodation and residency readiness

Lawful capacity, water/waste capacity and cash operating assumptions shape the stay model.

- [ ] T38 Define accommodation types, durations and lawful capacity
- [ ] T39 Collect accommodation demand and full operating cost inputs
- [ ] T40 Draft temporary accommodation and residency business plan
- [ ] T41 Build cash scenarios and funding-gap register
- [ ] T42 Prepare visitor terms and operating handbook
- [ ] T43 Prepare emergency plan, risk register and insurance scope
