Status: To prepare | Owner: Founding group | Version: 0.1 | Date: 10 October 2026

- accounts
- bank signatories

- opening cash and obligations

- [ ] Bank resolutions

- [ ] restricted funds
- [ ] invoices

- [ ] approvals
